For a long time, the answer to St. Lucia was simple: book the
all-inclusive on the north shore, and call it. The food was fine.
The pool was big. You came home with a tan.

We stopped sending clients there about four years ago.

## What changed

A small constellation of private villas opened up on the hillsides
above Soufrière — the village at the foot of the Pitons. Owner-run.
Three to six bedrooms. A chef who comes in for the meals you want and
disappears for the meals you don't. A pool that frames the twin
peaks better than any postcard ever has.

The price-per-night, for a family of six or eight, is competitive
with two adjacent suites at the resort. The experience is not.

## A typical week

- **Day 1.** Land in UVF, drive ninety minutes south through the rain
  forest. Settle in. Sunset on the terrace.
- **Day 2.** Sail day. A captained catamaran from the bay below the
  villa, north toward Marigot.
- **Day 3.** A morning hike up Gros Piton with a local guide. Long
  lunch back at the villa. Nap.
- **Day 4.** Drive to the south coast for a private chef dinner at
  one of the small fishing-village restaurants we love.
- **Day 5–6.** Beach, snorkeling, mud baths at Sulphur Springs.
- **Day 7.** Out the door at dawn, a hibiscus on the pillow.

- Real walking shoes for the Piton trail.
- A book you've been meaning to finish.

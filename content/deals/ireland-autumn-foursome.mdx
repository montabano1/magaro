The trip we send the most. Five days, three of the best courses on
the island, the manor at the lighthouse. A package we've negotiated
under our partner code for autumn 2026.

## The itinerary

**Day 1.** Arrive Shannon, drive to Cork. Settle in at our preferred
manor. Pub dinner.

**Day 2.** Old Head Golf Links. Two rounds available; most clients
choose one, with the second nine extended via the late-light option.

**Day 3.** Drive to Lahinch. Eighteen at Lahinch Old Course. Pints
in the village.

**Day 4.** Ballybunion. The Cashen if you've earned it. Dinner at
Adare Manor — a different evening on purpose.

**Day 5.** A morning round (optional) or a long lunch. Home from
Shannon.

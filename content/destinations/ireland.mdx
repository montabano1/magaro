Southwest Ireland is the trip we send when a client wants the trip
they'll measure all future golf trips against. The courses you've
heard of — Old Head, Ballybunion, Lahinch, Waterville, Tralee — sit
within driving distance of one another, and the hotels in between
are some of the finest in Europe. This is a one-of-one routing we've
refined over twenty-five years of bringing clients here.

## The week we build

Eight nights, three bases: a manor on the cliff at Old Head, a country
house in Killarney, and a final two nights at Adare for the room with
the view of the river. Six rounds, paced. A full day off in the middle
for a private guide through the Ring of Kerry, or a boat across to the
Skelligs if the weather holds.

We move clients by private driver — not because it's a luxury but
because the roads are narrow, the tee times are tight, and you should
be looking at the country, not the map.

## What makes it work

The wind. The light at four o'clock in the afternoon when you're on
the seventeenth at Old Head and the lighthouse goes very quiet. The
small bar at Tralee where the local farmer will tell you which line
to take on the par-three sixteenth. The peat fire at the manor when
the round runs over. None of these can be booked online. All of them
are why we send clients here.

## Who it suits

Buddies trips of four to twelve. Couples who play. Multigenerational
groups where the grandparents want country house and the kids want
links — Ireland holds both at once.

## When to plan

Tee times open eight to ten months out at the major courses. Adare
Manor and the manor at Old Head book further ahead. We start booking
October trips the previous January.

Sand Valley is the most important new golf destination in America in
the last twenty years, and it's still off the radar of most clients.
Five courses on a piece of restored Wisconsin barrens, every one of
them walking-only, every one of them designed by an architect at the
top of the discipline. We've sent groups here every year since the
resort opened, and the trip we build does not look like the resort's
default package.

## The shape of the trip

Three nights, four rounds. Fly into Madison or Milwaukee, drive a
private car the rest of the way. The Lido is the centerpiece — a
faithful re-creation of the C.B. Macdonald original on Long Island —
but Mammoth Dunes plays harder and the par-three Sandbox course is the
most fun afternoon golf in the country. We typically save Sandbox for
the last evening, after the long-format rounds are behind you.

## Why walking-only matters

Sand Valley is one of the few American resorts where every round is
walked, with a caddie. This is not a hardship for a fit golfer — the
land is flat, the courses are routed beautifully — and it changes the
trip. The pace is slower. The conversations are longer. The score is
incidental.

## Who it suits

Buddies trips of four to twelve. The resort handles big groups well.
Couples can come, but the spa and dining options are limited compared
to a coastal resort — be honest about expectations on the first call.
Not a multigenerational destination.

## When to plan

Six to nine months for prime weeks (June, July, September). Tee times
open online a year ahead but the resort holds inventory for our partner
rates at certain times.
